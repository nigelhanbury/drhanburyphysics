The diagram below, represents all 3 components connected in series, and is known as an LCR circuit. These circuits are extremely useful as they are used in the tuning circuits of radios and TVs etc. They also exhibit resonance, which we will examine later.

The above phasor and graphs show the relationship between VC, VR, and VL along with their corresponding phase difference. Notice, that there is a π radian, or 180° phase difference between VC and VL. Remember to use CIVIL to help construct the phasor.
