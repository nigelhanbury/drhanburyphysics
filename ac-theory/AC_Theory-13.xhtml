Option A: AC Theory

Increasing AC Supply Frequency

Increasing the frequency f, will result in an increase in the inductive reactance XL as X  L = 2πfL . Therefore, the peak and rms current in the circuit will decrease. The increase in frequency, results in a greater rate of change of current, and hence, a greater rate of change of flux linkage within the inductor. Owing to Lenz’s Law, this results in a greater induced emf across the inductor, which further impedes the current.

Increasing the Inductance

Increasing the inductance L, results in an increase of the inductive reactance XL. As before, this will decrease the rms and peak current in the circuit. Different inductors have differing numbers of turns of wire and cross sectional areas. This results in differing values of inductance, where some inductors will produce a greater induced emf for the same rate of change of flux linkage. Therefore, increasing L, will result in a greater induced emf for the same rate of change current. The greater induced emf will further impede the current.
