Option A: AC Theory

As the frequency approaches zero, f → 0, the current approaches zero. Referring to the key formulae, XL approaches zero and XC tends to infinity, resulting in the overall impedance tending to infinity.

We can say that as the frequency tends to zero, the supply is tending towards a DC supply, where the capacitor will not allow a direct current to flow.

As the frequency tends towards infinity, XL tends to infinity and XC tends to zero. The reason for this, is that for very high frequencies, the rate of change of flux linkage within the inductor is so high, that the induced emf across the inductor will virtually prevent the current from passing through the inductor.

Analysis of The Resonance Curve

We know that there is a phase difference of π radians or 180° between VC and VL. However, at resonance, the reactance of both the capacitor XC and inductor XL are equal.

Therefore, VC and VL have equal peak voltages, and so, VC+VL=0. The below circuit and phasor may help visualise this.

In other words, they cancel out, resulting in the supply voltage being applied purely across the resistance R, only.
