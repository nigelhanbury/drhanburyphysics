The circuit below, represents part of a radio tuner. The AC supply is the output signal of an aerial. The frequency of the radio wave detected by the aerial will be the supply frequency to the circuit. The variable capacitor is connected to the tuning dial. To tune into a particular radio station, the resonant frequency of the circuit must match the radio station frequency. The Q value indicates how well the tuner can filter out other frequencies other than the one at resonance.

Determine the rms current at resonance.

When the tuning dial is turned, the capacitance is varied as shown in the circuit. Determine the minimum and maximum resonant frequencies obtained, and hence, the range of radio stations that can be detected.

Determine the Q value at the above frequencies and comment on your answer.

Explain why the Q value changes in the way it has above.

State one simple change to the circuit that will increase the Q values.

Solution

At resonance, the impedance is purely resistive, and so, Z = R. Therefore, I  rms =  5.0 _ 5.0  = 1.0mA .

We can use f  0 =  1 ____ 2π √ _ LC    .

f  0min =  1  _____________________   2π ×  √ ___________________    (5.0 ×  10  −6 )  ×  (20.0 ×  10  −9 )     = 503kHz and f  0max =  1  _____________________   2π ×  √ ___________________    (5.0 ×  10  −6 )  ×  (2.0 ×  10  −9 )     = 1.59MHz .

Therefore, the radio station range is 503kHz → 1.59MHz.

We can use Q =  2π f  0 L ___ R  . At 503kHz, Q  503kHz =  2π ×  (503 ×  10  3 )  ×  (5.0 ×  10  −6 )    ___________________  5.0  = 3.16 .

At 1.59MHz, Q  503kHz =  2π ×  (1.59 ×  10  6 )  ×  (5.0 ×  10  −6 )    ___________________  5.0  = 10.0 .

The larger Q value at 1.59MHz indicates that the circuit is more effective at filtering out the other frequencies around 1.59MHz.

From Q =  2π f  0 L ___ R  , we can see that Q ∝ f  0 . Therefore, as the resonant frequency increases, the Q value increases.

As Q ∝  1 _ R  , decreasing the resistance R, will increase the Q value.
