The circuit shows a 0.15Ω resistor connected in series with a 50mF capacitor. The circuit is connected to an AC supply with an rms voltage of 6.0V and frequency 50Hz.

Determine the reactance of the capacitor, and hence, the impedance of the circuit.

Determine the rms current within the circuit.

Determine the mean power dissipated within the circuit.

Determine the rms voltage across both components.

Solution

Using X  C =  1 _ 2πfC  , we obtain the reactance X  C =  1 ____________  2π × 50 × 50 ×  10  −3   = 0.0637Ω .

The impedance Z, is given by Z = √ ______  X  C  2   +  R  2   . Therefore, Z = √ _________   0.0637  2  +  0.15  2   = 0.163Ω .

The rms current is given by I  rms =  6.0 _ 0.163  = 36.8A .

Since the mean power transferred to the capacitor is zero, it follows that the mean power dissipated within the circuit must be dissipated within the resistor.

Therefore, we can use <P> = I  rms  2  R , which gives <P> = 36.8  2 × 0.15 = 203W .

Using V  Rrms = I  rms R , gives V  Rrms = 36.8 × 0.15 = 5.5V .

Using V  Crms = I  rms X  C , gives V  Crms = 36.8 × 0.0637 = 2.3V .
