The component of the magnetic field B, perpendicular to the cross sectional area of the coil is given by Bcos(ωt), where ω, represents the angular velocity of the rotating coil, and is also expressed as ω = 2πf , where f, is the rotational frequency of the coil.

The magnetic flux linkage at any instant is given by Nϕ = BANcos(ωt) , where N represents the number of turns of wire making up the coil, and A, being the cross sectional area of the coil.
