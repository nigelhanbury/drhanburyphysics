Since I = I  0 sin (ωt) and P = I  2 R , the power at any instant is given by P = I  0  2 R sin  2 (ωt) . It is more useful to use the mean power dissipated <P> . Note the use of < > . This is a common symbol that represents the mean value, as discussed in unit 3.3 ‘Kinetic Theory’.

The mean power is given by <P> = < I  2 R> = < I  2 > R . The term < I  2 > is the mean square of the current I. The shaded area on the above graph represents the energy dissipated over one period (or cycle). The mean power is simply the energy dissipated over one cycle divided by the period T.
