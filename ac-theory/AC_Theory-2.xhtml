The graphs show how the pd V, and current I, vary sinusoidally with time as the coil rotates between the poles of a magnet. Notice that V and I oscillate in phase as V ∝ I .

They both reach their peak values when the coil is parallel to the field, and zero when perpendicular.

It is important to recognise that the pd and current are in phase for a resistor, as later, we will discover that they are not, when an alternating emf is applied across a capacitor or inductor.

The term AC, refers to Alternating Current, where the current varies sinusoidally.
