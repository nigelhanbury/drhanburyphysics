Useful Acronym - CIVIL

It can be difficult remembering the phase relationship between current and voltage for a capacitor and inductor. The acronym, CIVIL can be very useful. For a capacitor (C), I leads V and V leads I for an inductor (L). Every time you have to solve a problem, make it a habit of writing CIVIL in the margin.
