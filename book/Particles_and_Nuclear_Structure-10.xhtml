Fundamental interaction responsible for any particular interaction

Generally, the fundamental interaction responsible for any particular interaction between particles is the strongest one, which is felt by all the particles on both sides of the equation.

Fundamental interaction

Properties

Strong

The particles involved in the interaction, both before and after, are made up of quarks and belong to the category of hadrons.

There is no alteration in the type of quarks present in the particles.

The particles have a very brief lifetime and decay within approximately 10  -24 seconds.

Electromagnetic (EM)

One or multiple photons are released during the interaction.

All the particles involved either carry an electric charge or contain charged constituents.

There is no alteration in the flavour of quarks present in the particles.

The interactions have a short lifespan ranging from approximately 10  -18 seconds to 10  -12 seconds.

Weak

These observations are applicable in cases where the strong and/or electromagnetic forces are not in effect.

Neutrinos are only affected by the weak force, which explains why they have such feeble interactions with matter. Therefore, any interaction involving neutrinos must be regulated by the weak force.

The weak force is the only force that has the capability of modifying the flavour of quarks.

The interactions in this scenario have a relatively prolonged lifespan, lasting approximately 10^-10 seconds or more.
