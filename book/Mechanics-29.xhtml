1.3 Dynamics

Up to now, we have examined motion in some detail. This section deals with the causes behind motion and its changes. Therefore, forces will be examined in detail.

Newton’s Laws of Motion

Newton’s First Law: A body will either remain at rest or continue with uniform velocity unless acted upon by a resultant force.

Newton’s Second Law: The rate of change of momentum of an object is proportional to the resultant force acting on it, and takes place in the direction of that force.

Newton’s Third Law: If a body A exerts a force on a body B, then body B exerts an equal and opposite force back on body A.

The above laws will be examined in detail as we proceed through this section and are commonly referred to as N1, N2 and N3 respectively.
