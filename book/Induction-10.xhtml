Solution

In position A, the flux linkage is zero as no lines of flux pass through the cross sectional area of the coil. Since the lines of flux are perpendicular to the cross sectional area of the coil in position B, we can write Nɸ = BAN = 0.10 × (20.0 × 30.0)   − 4 × 200 = 1.2Wb .

Time taken for coil position to change is one quarter of a cycle. Period, T =  1 _ 50  = 0.020s . Therefore, Δt =  0.020 _ 4  = 5.0ms .

Using Faraday’s Law, ε = −  Δ (Nɸ)  ___ Δt  = −  1.2 _____ 5.0 ×  10   − 3   = − 240V . Magnitude is 240V.

The mean EMF induced is zero as the total change in flux linkage is zero over one cycle.

Since there are N turns of wire, the induced EMF across the left side of the coil is given by ε = NBLv .

We can use v = rω to determine velocity at which left side of the coil cuts field. Therefore, v = (10.0 ×  10   − 2 ) × 2π × 50 = 31.4m s   − 1 .

Using ε = NBLv , gives ε = 200 × 0.10 × 0.300 × 31.4 = 188V .

Since the right side of the coil is moving through the field in the reverse direction, an EMF in the reverse direction will be induced across the right side. Therefore, the total EMF across the coil is 188 × 2 = 376V.
