Progressive Waves

Both transverse and progressive waves are known as progressive waves, in which, the wave profile can be seen to propagate (move forward), and so, transfers energy in the direction of wave travel.

A progressive wave is a transfer of energy, without the transfer of the matter, as a result of oscillations of the medium through which energy is travelling. A progressive wave is energy in motion. In a progressive wave there is a net transfer of energy from one point to another.

Definition of Displacement

The displacement of any particle of the wave is its distance in a given direction from the rest or equilibrium position. It is a vector quantity.

Definition of Amplitude

The amplitude is the magnitude of the maximum displacement of any particle on the wave from its equilibrium or rest position. It is a scalar quantity.

Definition of Period

The period is the time taken (in seconds) for any particle on the wave to perform one complete cycle.

Definition of Frequency

The frequency of a wave is the number of cycles of a wave that pass a given point in one second or alternatively, the frequency of a wave is the number of cycles of oscillation per second performed by any particle in the medium through which the wave is passing. SI unit of frequency is the hertz, symbol HZ, where 1HZ = 1s-1
