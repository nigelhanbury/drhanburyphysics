A cylinder with a leak proof piston at one end contains 0.50mol of an ideal gas .

The gas is taken around the cycle (1 − 2 − 3 − 4 − 1 ), and acts as a heat engine.

Determine:

The change in temperature going from 1 to 2.

The quantity of heat transfer going from 1 to 2 and state its direction.

The quantity of heat transfer going from 2 to 3, 3 to 4 and 4 to 1.

The net work done by the gas

The efficiency of the heat engine is given by, efficiency = (  net work done by the engine  ___________  heat absorbed by the engine  ) × 100%

Calculate the efficiency of the heat engine.

Solution

Using PV = nRT , (4.0 ×  10  5 ) × (1.0 ×  10  −2 ) = 0.50 × 8.31 × T  1 ⇒ T  1 = 963K and (2.5 ×  10  5 ) × (1.5 ×  10  −2 ) = 0.50 × 8.31 × T  2 ⇒ T  2 = 903K . Therefore, ΔT = − 60K .

Using ΔU =  3 _ 2  nRΔT =  3 _ 2  × 0.50 × 8.31 × (− 60) = − 374J and work equals area under line, gives W =  1 _ 2  × (4.0 + 2.5) × 10  5 × (0.5 ×  10  −2 ) = 1630J . Therefore, we can use ΔU = Q − W .

Therefore, − 374 = Q − 1630 ⇒ Q = 1260J . This means that 1260J of heat was absorbed by the engine.

Part 2 to 3: We can use ΔU =  3 _ 2  VΔP =  3 _ 2  × (1.5 ×  10  −2 ) × (− 1.0 ×  10  5 ) = − 2250J and W = 0 , since no change in volume. Therefore, we can use ΔU = Q − W , giving − 2250 = Q − 0 ⇒ Q = − 2250J

This means that 2250J of heat was removed from the engine.

Part 3 to 4: Using PV = nRT , (1.5 ×  10  5 ) × (1.5 ×  10  −2 ) = 0.50 × 8.31 × T  3 ⇒ T  3 = 542K and (3.0 ×  10  5 ) × (1.0 ×  10  −2 ) = 0.50 × 8.31 × T  4 ⇒ T  4 = 722K . Therefore, ΔT = 180K .

Using ΔU =  3 _ 2  nRΔT =  3 _ 2  × 0.50 × 8.31 × (180) = 1120J and work equals area under line, gives W = −  1 _ 2  × (3.0 + 1.5) × 10  5 × (0.5 ×  10  −2 ) = − 1130J . Therefore, we can use ΔU = Q − W .

Therefore, 1120 = Q − (− 1130) ⇒ Q = − 10J . This means that 10J of heat was removed from the engine.

Part 4 to 1: We can use ΔU =  3 _ 2  VΔP =  3 _ 2  × (1.0 ×  10  −2 ) × (1.0 ×  10  5 ) = 1500J and W = 0 , since no change in volume. Therefore, we can use ΔU = Q − W , giving 1500 = Q − 0 ⇒ Q = 1500J

This means that 1500J of heat was absorbed by the engine.

Net work done by engine equals enclosed area, and so, W = (1.0 ×  10  5 ) × (0.50 ×  10  −2 ) = 500J . (Area of trapezium)

Using efficiency = (  net work done by the engine  ___________  heat absorbed by the engine  ) × 100% , gives efficiency = (  500 ___________ 1260 + 1500  ) × 100% = 18.1% .
