Example Problem

The gravitational field strength at the surface of the Earth is 9.81Nkg − 1 and has a radius of 6400km.

Determine:

The gravitational field strength at a distance of 38400km from the Earth’s centre.

The gravitational force acting on a space vehicle of mass 700kg at the above distance.

Solution

We can make use of the inverse square law, g ∝  1 __  r  2   and let R = 6400km .

We can express the distance in terms of R.  38400 _ 6400  = 6.0 .

Therefore, 38400km = 6R . This leads to a field strength, g =  9.81 ___   6.0  2   = 0.27N kg   − 1 .

Using F = mg = 700 × 0.27 = 190N .
