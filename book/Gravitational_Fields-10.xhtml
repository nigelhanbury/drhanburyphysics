Solution

The sketch is shown on the right. Key points have been labelled.

The magnitude of the gradient must decrease as distance from Earth increases as field strength decreases. The gradient is positive as the field strength acts to the left.

When the field strength is zero, the gradient must be zero.

The magnitude of the gradient must then increase, but more slowly, as the field strength is generally smaller. The sign of the gradient must be negative as the field now acts to the right.

The potential must be below the horizontal axis at all times, as the potential will always be negative.
