Line of Action of Force Passing Through Stated Point

The diagram on the right, represents decreasing the angle, θ while keeping the force, F constant. As can be seen, the distance, d decreases as θ decreases.

It is easily seen, that as θ approaches zero, the distance, d approaches zero. At this point, the line of action of the force (dotted line) passes straight through the stated point.

Since d = 0, the moment about the stated point, Fd = 0.

This is a very important point. If the line of action of the force passes through the stated point, then the moment of the force about that stated point is zero. This is a useful fact when solving static problems. More on this later.

If the line of action of the force passes through the stated point, then the moment of the force about that stated point is zero.

Moment about Stated Point using Components of Force

The force, F, on the right can be resolved into perpendicular components using the parallelogram rule as shown. Remember that the force, F can now be replaced with these components, FV and FH. The line of action of the component, FH passes through the stated point, and hence, the moment of FH about the stated point is zero and makes no contribution to the net moment.

Since FV is perpendicular to L, the net moment of the force, F, about the stated point is given by FV L. We know that FV = Fsinθ, and so, the net moment can be expressed as FLsinθ.
