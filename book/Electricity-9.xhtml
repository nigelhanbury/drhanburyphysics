Practical Investigation

The apparatus should be set up as shown in the diagram.

An ice water mixture can be used to record the resistance at a temperature of 0°C

The water bath should be heated (e.g. with a Bunsen burner) and the water stirred continuously in order to ensure an even temperature throughout the water bath.

Once the required temperature has been reached remove the heat source and record the reading of resistance on the ohmmeter OR take the ammeter and voltmeter readings and calculate the resistance using the formula R=V/I.

This process should be repeated at 10°C intervals until the water boils (100°C).

Repeat the measurement of temperature and resistance during cooling.

Plot a graph of resistance (y-axis) against temperature (x-axis). This should be a straight line, but NOT through the origin.
