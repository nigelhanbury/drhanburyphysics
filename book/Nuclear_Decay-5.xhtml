Background Radiation

Radiation is all around us. The pie chart on the right represents a breakdown of the main sources. Radon gas provides the largest contribution, and is produced naturally in some types of rocks. Man-made sources include, x-rays, nuclear power etc. Surprisingly, food and drink are sources of radiation, including some building materials.

Dangers of Ionising Radiation

All three types of radiation cause ionisation, with alpha causing the greatest and gamma, the least. Ionising cells can lead to cell mutation, and increase the risk of cancer. An alpha source outside the body is relatively safe, due to its short range in air and the skin over your body being able to block it. However, an alpha source inside your body, is extremely harmful as it will cause the greatest ionisation of the cells within your body.
