A transverse wave is one where the particle oscillations are at right angles to the direction of travel of the wave.

A longitudinal wave is one where the particle oscillations are in line with (parallel to) the direction of travel (or propagation) of the wave.

Diagram A, represents a displacement-distance graph, which shows a whole wave as it progresses.

Diagram B, represents a displacement-time graph, which shows how the displacement of a single particle on the wave varies with time.
