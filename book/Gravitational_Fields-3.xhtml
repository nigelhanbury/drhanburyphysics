Gravitational field strength, g / Nkg − 1

Consider the Earth and Moon as shown in the diagram on the right. At each point between the Earth and Moon, there will be a resultant gravitational field strength equal to the vector addition of the field strength contribution from the Earth and Moon.

At some point, the resultant gravitational field strength will be zero as the field strength due to the Earth will be equal in magnitude but opposite in direction to that of the Moon. Once a space vehicle is beyond this point, the Moon’s gravitational field will be dominant and pull the vehicle towards it.

Notice that the curve passes through the zero axis, indicating that the direction of the resultant field strength has changed.

As the gravitational field strength at the surface of the Moon is less than that at the surface of the Earth, g  m < g  E .
