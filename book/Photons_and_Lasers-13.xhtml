Photon Momentum

We know that light behaves as particles, and as such, we can attribute momentum to a photon.

Because of this, light will exert a force (or pressure) on a surface that the light is incident upon. Newton’s second law states that the force is proportional to the rate of change of momentum. We can use this to calculate such pressure.
