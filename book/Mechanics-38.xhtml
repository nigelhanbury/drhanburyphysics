The Principle of Conservation of Energy

Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transferred from one form to another. Energy is a scalar.

The total energy within a system must be constant. An example of this is when a ball is falling under gravity. The decrease in gravitational potential energy equals the increase in kinetic energy.
