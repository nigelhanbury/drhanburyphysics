We can express the above equation in a much simpler form, but is not stated in the formula booklet for WJEC.

Work Required For Orbital Manoeuvre

Sometimes it is necessary for a spacecraft to gain altitude from a lower planetary orbit to a higher planetary orbit. One such manoeuvre is called the Hohmann transfer. This type of manoeuvre is used to transfer between two circular orbits. Engine thrust is provided at point A to send the craft into an elliptical orbit. Further thrust is applied at point B to send the craft into a higher circular orbit.

The work done by the engine, W is equal to the gain in total energy.

In other words, W = Δ E  T = −  1 _ 2  × GMm (  1 __  r  2    −   1 __  r  1   ) .
