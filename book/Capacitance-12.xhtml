Determining Time Constant for a Discharging Capacitor

Gradient is given by −  1 _ CR 

By measuring the pd V, across a discharging capacitor over equally spaced time intervals, the time constant of the circuit can be determined.

Using V = V  0 e   −   t _ CR  , we can take the natural log of both sides to give

lnV = − (  1 _ CR  ) t + ln V  0 .

By plotting lnV against t, and comparing the above equation with the equation of a straight line y = mx + c , it can be seen that the time constant CR, can be determined from the measurement of the gradient of the graph.

Note, as lnV against t is linear, proves that the pd V, must decay exponentially.
