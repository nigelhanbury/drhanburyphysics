In-Phase and Anti-Phase

Diagram 1, represents two particles separated by one wavelength, λ. As can be seen, the two particles are travelling in the same direction at the same speed. Therefore, they occupy the same point within a cycle. These particles are said to oscillate in phase. Diagram 2, represents two particles separated by half a wavelength. These particles are travelling in opposite directions at the same speed. We say that the particles oscillate in anti-phase, and have a phase difference of 180º or π radians.

Definition of Wavelength

The wavelength of a progressive wave is the minimum distance, measured along the direction of propagation, between two points on the wave oscillating in phase.

Definition of Wave Speed

The speed of a wave is the distance that the wave profile moves per unit time. This is the same as the speed at which the energy moves, or a crest moves.

Derivation of Wave Speed

In a time equal to one period, the wave will move forward one wavelength.
