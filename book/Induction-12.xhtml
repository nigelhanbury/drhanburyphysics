Unit 4, Section 5: Electromagnetic Induction

Example Problem

Conducting wire loop

Field, B acting out of page and decreasing

The diagram on the right, shows a conducting wire loop placed within a magnetic field acting out of the page. The field strength is decreasing. Determine the direction of the induced current and explain your answer.

Solution

The decreasing field strength is causing the magnetic flux linkage to decrease. The induced current will be in the anti - clockwise direction using Lenz’s Law. This current will produce a magnetic field out of the page in an attempt to prevent the flux linkage from decreasing.
