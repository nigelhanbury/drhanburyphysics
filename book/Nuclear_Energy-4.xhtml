Unit 3, Section 6: Nuclear Energy

Example Problem

Determine the binding energy of Carbon - 12,  6  12  C . Mass data is provided below (1u = 931.5MeV).

1  1  p = 1.007276u

0  1  n = 1.008665u

− 1  0  e = 0.000549u

Solution

By definition, the mass of a Carbon - 12 atom is 12u.

Therefore, we must determine the mass of the nucleus by subtracting the 6 orbital electrons.

We can write m  NUCLEUS = 12u − (6 × 0.000549u) = 11.996706u .

Total mass of constituent nucleons, m  CONSTITUENTS = (6 × 1.007276) + (6 × 1.008665) = 12.095646u .

Mass defect, Δm = m  CONSTITUENTS − m  NUCLEUS , therefore, Δm = 12.095646 − 11.996706 = 0.09894u .

Therefore, the binding energy is BE = 0.09894 × 931.5 = 92.2MeV .

Example Problem

Thorium - 232,  90  232  Th decays by alpha emission to form Radium - 228,  88  228  Ra .

The decay is represented as  90  232  Th →  88  228  Ra +   2  4  He .

Determine the energy released in this decay.

Mass of Thorium atom = 232.0380553u

Mass of Helium atom = 4.002603u

Mass of Radium atom = 228.0310703u

Solution

Alpha particle, 2  4  He

 90  232  Th

 88  228  Ra

Mass of Thorium nucleus is given by 232.0380553 − (90 × 0.000549) = 231.9886453u .

Mass of Helium nucleus is given by 4.002603 − (2 × 0.000549) = 4.001505u .

Mass of Radium nucleus is given by 228.0310703 − (88 × 0.000549) = 227.9827583u .

Total mass of products is given by 227.9827583 + 4.001505 = 231.9842633u .

Mass defect, Δm = 231.9886453 − 231.9842633 = 0.004382u .

Energy released is given by E = 0.004382 × 931.5 = 4.08MeV .
