2.6 The Refraction of Light

The diagram on the right, represents plane wavefronts passing from medium 1 to medium 2. As the wavefronts pass across the boundary, the wave speed changes. In this particular example, the wave speed decreases. As can be seen, the direction of wave travel changes, indicated by the light rays changing direction. On close inspection, it is easily seen that the wavelength of the light decreases as the wavefronts pass into medium 2. The frequency of the light waves does not change when passing from one medium to another.

Definition of Snell’s Law

Snell’s Law states that at the boundary between any two given materials, the ratio of the sine of the angle of incidence to the sine of the angle of refraction is a constant.

The phenomenon of waves changing direction when passing from one medium to another due to a change in wave speed is known as refraction.
