Example Problem

Plutonium - 238 is a very strong alpha emitter and is able to generate significant quantities of thermal power via the natural radioactive decay process. This makes it an ideal power source for space missions. These devices are known as Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generators (or RTG). Typically, the mass of Plutonium - 238 used is 4.50kg. The energy released in the decay of Plutonium - 238 is 5.59MeV. The half - life of Plutonium - 238 is 87.7 years.

Determine:

The number of Plutonium nuclei present

The activity

The thermal power generated

Solution

The number of moles present is given by n =  M __  M  m    =  4.50 _______  (238 ×  10   − 3 )   = 18.9mol .

Number of nuclei is given by N = n N  A = 18.9 × 6.02 × 10  23 = 1.14 × 10  25 .

The decay constant is given by 𝝀 =  ln2 ___  T    1 _ 2    =  ln2 ___   (87.7 × 365 × 24 × 3600)   = 2.51 × 10   − 10 s   − 1 .

Using A = 𝜆N , gives A = (2.51 ×  10   − 10 ) × (1.14 ×  10  25 ) = 2.86 × 10  15 Bq .

The thermal power is given by P = (2.86 ×  10  15 ) × (5.59 ×  10  6  × 1.6 ×  10   − 19 ) = 2560W .

Example Problem

Nuclear fission was used in the early nuclear weapons and released a tremendous quantity of energy. The blast yield was measured in terms of the equivalent energy released in 1 tonne of TNT explosive. 1 tonne of TNT = 4.18 × 10  12 J .

Determine the blast yield produced by 1.0kg of Uranium - 235. Assume that all nuclei undergo fission.

Energy is given by (2.6 ×  10  24 ) × (180 ×  10  6  × 1.6 ×  10   − 19 ) = 7.5 × 10  13 J .
