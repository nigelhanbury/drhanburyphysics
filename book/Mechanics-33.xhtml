Free Body Diagrams

The diagram on the right, represents a free body diagram. This is simply an object or system that has all the relevant vector forces added, such that the dynamics of the body or system can be analysed.

Here, there is a mass, m sat inside a lift, which is accelerating upwards. In order to analyse the forces acting on the mass, we can separate the mass and the forces acting on that mass from the rest of the system as shown. Now it is much easier to analyse the forces on that mass.
