Electric Field Strength between Two Charged Parallel Plates

d

A uniform electric field is obtained between two parallel metal plates, which are a distance d, apart and have a potential difference V, between them.

The field lines are:

Parallel to each other and equally spaced due to uniform field strength

Perpendicular to the metal plates

Directed from the positive plate to the negative plate

The field strength is increased by:

Increasing the potential difference, V

Decreasing the plate separation, d

The field strength E, is given by E =  V _ d  . Units: Vm − 1 .

This is also known as the potential gradient between the two plates, where this will be discussed in more detail in the next section.

The work done by the field W, in moving this test charge a distance d, between the plates is W = Fd = qEd .

The potential difference V, across the plates is defined as the work done per unit positive charge in moving the charge across the plates.

Therefore, V =  W _ q  =  qEd _ q  = Ed . This then leads to the equation E =  V _ d  .
