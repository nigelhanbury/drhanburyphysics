2.4 The Nature of Waves

The simplest type of wave to talk about is a wave on a rope, which we are all familiar with. In the example shown on the right, one end of the rope is sharply displaced in a direction perpendicular to the rope. As can be seen, a disturbance propagates along the rope in the direction shown. The shape of this disturbance is known as the wave profile. The velocity at which the wave profile travels is known as the velocity of the wave.

Definition of Transverse Wave

A transverse wave is one where the particle oscillations are at right angles to the direction of travel of the wave.

Transverse Waves

A transverse wave is one where the particle oscillations are at right angles to the direction of travel of the wave.

Transverse waves are made up of peaks and troughs

Using the example of the rope, each particle that makes up the rope will oscillate in the direction shown by the double ended arrow.

The diagram on the right, represents two transverse waves where their direction of oscillations are perpendicular to each other.

Longitudinal Waves

A longitudinal wave is one where the particle oscillations are in line with (parallel to) the direction of travel (or propagation) of the wave.

These waves are made up of compressions and rarefactions.

Definition of Longitudinal Wave
