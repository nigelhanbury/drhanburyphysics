The absorption spectra of a distant star is shown on the right. The maximum red and blue shifted spectra are shown only, along with the coresponding unchanged spectra. The time interval between the measured spectra was 5 years.

Determine the recessional velocity of the star - planet system

Determine the orbital velocity of the star about the common centre of mass

Determine the orbital period of the star about the common centre of mass

Solution

First, we need to work out the max and min radial velocities using v  rad = (  c _  λ  ) Δλ .

Therefore, v  max = (  3.0 ×  10  8  _______ 650 ×  10   − 9   ) × (3.0 ×  10   − 14 ) = 13.8m s   − 1 , and v  min = (  3.0 ×  10  8  _______ 650 ×  10   − 9   ) × (− 1.5 ×  10   − 14 ) = − 6.90m s   − 1 .

The recessional velocity is then given by v  rec =  13.8 +  (− 6.90)   _______ 2  = 3.45m s   − 1 .

The orbital velocity is given by v  o = v  max − v  rec = 13.8 − 3.45 = 10.4m s   − 1 .

The orbital period is 10 years.
