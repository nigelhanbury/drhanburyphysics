Determination of Young Modulus

The apparatus is shown below.

Measurements

Use a micrometer to measure the diameter of wire at different positions along its length and then find a mean.

Use a metre rule to measure the original length of wire (i.e. the unloaded length).

Use the mm scale to find the position of the marker for each known load or mass.

Calculations

Force applied = mass × g

Extension = scale reading with load – scale reading without load

Cross sectional area of wire, A =  π d  2  __ 4  , where d is the diameter

Strain, ε =  Δl __  l  0   , where ∆l is the extension and l0 is the original length

Stress, σ =  F _ A  , where F is the force and A is the cross sectional area.

Analysis

Plot a graph of stress against strain

This should give a straight line that passes through the origin, provided that the limit of proportionality has not been exceeded.

The gradient of the graph is equal to the Young modulus of the material.
