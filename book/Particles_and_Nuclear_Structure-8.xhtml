Example Problem

Another example of beta decay is called beta plus decay. This involves the decay of Polonium into Silicon with an emission of a positron and electron neutrino.

15  30  Po → 14  30  Si +  e  +  +  ν  e

Explain why on this occasion, an electron neutrino is emitted and not an anti-electron neutrino.

State what quark flavour change occurs if any.

Solution

p → n + e  + + ν  e

Since the lepton number for the positron is -1, an electron neutrino with lepton number of +1 must be created in order that the lepton count is conserved.

(u u d) → (d d u) + e  + + ν  e

u → d + e  + + ν  e

Hence, the up quark changed to a down quark.
