Laser Construction

Amplifying medium

In the amplifying medium (a gas), the conditions are right for stimulated emission. One photon causes emission of two photos, that then cause emission of four photons etc. This leads to an exponential increase in energy output.

100% reflecting mirror

Mirrors at both ends of the amplifying medium are used to reflect the light. In stimulated emission, one photon causes emission of two photons, and this event repeats in the amplifying medium. When the light is reflected, the number of these events increases again. This ensures the beam will pass through the amplifying medium many times. Every time the beam passes, its intensity (power) increases exponentially.

99% reflecting mirror

The transmitting end of the laser has a mirror that is 99% reflecting. 1% of the light is transmitted out of the cavity. 99% of the light is reflected to the other mirror. This means that on average, each photon will pass back and forth through the amplifying medium 100 times before exiting.

Power of transmitted laser

The transmitted light from the partially reflecting mirror is only made from a fraction of the photons reaching that mirror every second. The power of the laser is the energy of each of these photons (depends on wavelength) times the number of photons transmitted
