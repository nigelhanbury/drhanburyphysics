Determination of Initial Current

Referring to the charging circuit on the right, we know that V  0 = V  c + V  R .

At the start of charging, the capacitor has zero charge, and so, V  C = 0 as V  C =  Q _ C  with Q = 0 . Therefore, V  R = V  o .

In other words, at the start of charging, the supply EMF is applied across only the resistor, R.

Therefore, the initial current is given by I  0 =  supply emf _ R  .

Determination of Final PD

At the end of charging, the current is zero. Therefore, V  R = 0 as V  R = IR . This leads to V  C = V  o . In other words, the final pd across the capacitor is equal to the supply emf. This also results in the final charge being, Q  0 = C × supply emf .
