Drag the magnet left and right to move it towards and away from the coil. The display shows the induced poles, current direction, and the force that opposes the magnet's motion (Lenz's Law).

🧲 Controls

📈 Information

Press Drop Magnet to release the magnet from rest, or drag the magnet downward to scrub through the fall manually. The data logger records the induced EMF as the magnet passes through the coil.

Adjust Drop height and Pole down to explore how these affect the EMF trace. Use the checkboxes to annotate pulse widths and heights.
