Help — Add Vectors
What this mode does
Build a sequence of vectors head-to-tail from the origin. The green resultant runs from the origin to the tip of the final vector — its magnitude and angle update live in the side panel.
Creating vectors
Click and drag from the origin to create the first vector. Each subsequent drag starts from the previous vector's tip, chaining them head-to-tail.
The canvas auto-fits to keep all vectors and the resultant in view.
Editing vectors
Drag a vector's tip to change its magnitude and direction. Vectors that follow it in the chain reposition automatically to stay head-to-tail.
Use the × button next to any vector in the list to remove it.
Display options
Show components — decomposes each vector into horizontal and vertical parts, drawn in separate lanes alongside the canvas.
Show resultant — toggles the green resultant arrow.
Show angles — labels the angle each vector makes with the horizontal.
Show values — appends numerical magnitudes to the labels.
Help — Forces
What this mode does
Apply multiple forces to a central mass and watch the resultant force (green) update live. Useful for equilibrium problems, concurrent forces, and the parallelogram law.
Creating forces
Drag outwards from the mass to add a force. The drag direction sets the force's line of action; the drag length sets its magnitude.
Editing forces
Drag a force's tip to change its magnitude and direction.
Drag a force's body to reposition it — useful for arranging forces head-to-tail away from the mass.
Use the × button next to any force in the list to remove it.
Parallelogram
Tick Show parallelogram, then tap any two forces to select them. The parallelogram of those two forces is drawn, with its diagonal showing their combined effect.
Tap a selected force again to deselect it.
Equilibrium indicator
When the resultant is effectively zero, a pulsing BALANCED label appears at the mass. Useful for showing students when forces are in equilibrium.
