Example Problem

A vessel contains compressed Helium-4 at a pressure of 6.0 × 10  5 Pa . The volume of the vessel is 3.0m3 and at a temperature of 25°C.

Determine:

The number of moles of Helium

The density of Helium under these conditions

The rms speed of the Helium atoms

Solution

Using PV = nRT , gives (6.0 ×  10  5 ) × 3.0 = n × 8.31 × (25 + 273.16) ⇒ n = 726mol

Recognising that Helium-4 has a molar mass of 4.0gmol −1, we can determine the total mass, M by M = 726 × (4.0 ×  10  −3 ) = 2.9kg

The density is given by ρ =  M _ V  =  2.9 _ 3.0  = 0.97kg m  -3

Using P =  1 _ 3  ρ _  c  2 , gives 6.0 × 10  5 =  1 _ 3  × 0.97 × _  c  2 ⇒ _  c  2 = 1.9 × 10  6

Therefore, c  rms = √ ______ 1.9 ×  10  6   = 1.4 × 10  3 m s  −1

Example Problem

An air bubble is rising up through a large water tank as shown. The pressure and temperature are displayed on the diagram. The number of air molecules is 2.0 × 10  20 (The molar mass of air is 28.8gmol −1).

Determine:

The number of moles of air

The volume of the bubble

The mass of the air

The rms speed of the air molecules

Solution

Number of moles, n =  N __  N  A   =  2.0 ×  10  20  _______ 6.02 ×  10  23   = 3.3 × 10  −4 mol

Using PV = nRT , gives (2.0 ×  10  5 ) × V = (3.3 ×  10  −4 ) × 8.31 × (4.0 + 273.17) ⇒ V = 3.8 × 10  −6 m  3

Mass of air is M = n M  m = (3.3 × 10  −4 ) × (28.8 ×  10  −3 ) = 9.5 × 10  −6 kg

We can use PV =  1 _ 3  Nm _  c  2 =  1 _ 3  M _  c  2 . Then, (2.0 ×  10  5 ) × (3.8 ×  10  −6 ) =  1 _ 3  × (9.5 ×  10  −6 ) × _  c  2 ⇒ _  c  2 = 2.4 × 10  5

Therefore, c  rms = √ ______ 2.4 ×  10  5   = 490m s  −1
