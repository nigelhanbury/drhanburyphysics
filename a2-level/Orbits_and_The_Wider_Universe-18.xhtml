Critical Density of the Universe

We can use Newton’s law of gravitation to investigate this question. We will consider the matter in a shell of space at a radius r, from our galaxy, G. The whole Universe can be considered to exist within the sphere of radius r, and be of uniform density. This is a reasonable assumption, as on this scale, the Universe can be considered homogenous.

Fate of the Universe conditions:

E > 0, the shell will expand forever.

E = 0, the shell will come to a halt at an infinite distance and the Universe will have a flat geometry.

E < 0, the shell will reach a maximum distance and then collapse.
