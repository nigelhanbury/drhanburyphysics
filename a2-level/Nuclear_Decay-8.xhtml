Unit 3, Section 5: Nuclear Decay

Example Problem

The Thorium nucleus  90  232  Th , occurs naturally in the Earth's crust and decays through a series of alpha and beta emissions, eventually forming the stable nucleus of Lead  82  208  Pb .

Show, that the total number of alpha and beta particles emitted is 10. Note, that here, only beta minus occurs.

Solution

The total change can be expressed as  90  232  Th →  82  208  Pb .

Only alpha emissions cause a change in nucleon (mass) number, A. Therefore, we can determine the number of alpha emissions.

Since the change in mass number for each alpha decay is − 4 , we can write ΔA  Total = 208 − 232 = − 24 , and so, the number of alpha particles is given by 𝛼 =   (− 24)  _____  (− 4)   = 6 .

Each of the 6 alpha emission causes the proton (atomic) number, Z to change by − 2 , and each beta emission causes Z to change by + 1.

The total change in Z, is given by ΔZ  Total = 82 − 90 = − 8 . Therefore, we can write − 8 = 6 × (− 2) + 𝛽 × (+ 1) ⇒ 𝛽 = 4 .

Since there are 6 alpha and 4 beta decays, there must be a total of 10 particles emitted.

Example Problem

In class, a teacher wanted to demonstrate to their students, which radiations, α, β or γ was emitted from a small radioactive source. The count rate was measured for different absorbers and recorded below.

Absorber

Count rate/s−1

Paper

5924

1mm Aluminium

5909

5cm Lead

347

None

9154

Determine which types of radiation are emitted from the sample.

Solution

After paper is inserted, there is a significant drop in count rate from 9154s−1 to 5924s−1. Therefore, alpha must be present as paper blocks alpha.

After the paper is removed and the Aluminium is inserted, there is no further significant drop (compared to paper) in count rate from 9154s−1 to 5909s−1. Therefore, beta is not present as Aluminium block beta.

After the Aluminium is removed and the Lead is inserted, there is a further significant drop (compared to Aluminium) in count rate from 9154s−1 to 347s−1. Therefore, gamma must be present as Lead blocks gamma.

Alpha and gamma are present only.
