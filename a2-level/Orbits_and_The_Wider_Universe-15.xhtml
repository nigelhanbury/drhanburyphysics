Hubble’s Law

Hubble analysed the absorption spectra of light from some relatively near galaxies and determined the red shift from his measurements. The distance to these galaxies from Earth were known. Using the Doppler shift equation, Hubble calculated the recessional speed of each of these galaxies. When he plotted recessional speed against distance from Earth, the result was a straight line through the origin.

His findings are summarized in Hubble’s Law, which states that the recessional speed of a galaxy is directly proportional to its distance from Earth.

This is expressed mathematically as v = H  0 D , where,

v = recessional speed of galaxy

D = distance from Earth

H  0 = Hubble constant

Therefore, the gradient of the graph on the right represents the Hubble constant.

On closer inspection of the graph, it can be seen, that for small distances from Earth, there is considerable scatter. This scatter is due to measuring the doppler shift of galxies within a galaxy cluster. In this situation, galaxies experience gravitational attraction and aquire additional velocities other than that caused by the expanding Universe. These are known as residual velocities. At much greater distances, these residual velocities are much smaller than the recessional velocity, and so, little scatter is observed.

1pc (parsec) is a common unit for galactic distances. 1pc = 3.26 light years or 3.086 × 1016m.

The latest value of H0 is 70 ± 2 kms − 1Mpc − 1. In other words, a galaxy at a distance of 1Mpc will have a recessional speed (moving away from Earth) at 70kms − 1. H0 is simply the gradient of the graph.

Hubble showed that the whole Universe is expanding. The distance between all points in the Universe is continually increasing. This means that the speed of recession between any two points must be directly proportional to their separation, with the constant of proportionality being H0. H0 is a property of the expansion and it has the same value at all points in the Universe. Hubble’s law is now used to calculate the distance to galaxies at the edge of the observable Universe.

Definition of Hubble’s Law

The recessional speed of a galaxy is directly proportional to its distance from Earth.
