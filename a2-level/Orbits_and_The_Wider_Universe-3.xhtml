We can use T  2 = (  4 π  2  __ GM  ) × r  3 , and recognise that  4 π  2  __ GM  is the gradient of the graph.

Example Problem

Io and Europa are two of the moons of Jupiter. The distance of Io from Jupiter is 422000km and has an orbital period of 1.77 days. Given that the orbital period of Europa is 3.55 days, determine the distance of Europa from Jupiter.

Solution

We can make use of Kepler’s Third Law, T  2 ∝ r  3 , and use the ratio (   T  Io  __  T  Europa   )  2 = (   r  Io  __  r  Europa   )  3 . As we are using ratios, it does not matter which units are used, as long as they are used consistently.

Therefore, (  1.77 _ 3.55  )  2 = (  422000 ______  r  Europa   )  3 , and leads to r  Europa = 671000km .
