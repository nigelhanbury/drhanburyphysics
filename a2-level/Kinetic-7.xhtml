Example Problem

A gas cylinder of volume 0.020m3 contains compressed air at a pressure of 2.0 × 10  5 Pa at a temperature of 22°C. (The molar mass of air is 28.8gmol −1)

Determine:

The mass of air in the cylinder

The new pressure in the cylinder when an additional 2.0m3 of air at 22°C at atmospheric pressure of 1.0 × 10  5 Pa is pumped into the cylinder. Assume no change in temperature during the process.

Solution

Firstly, we need to calculate the number of moles. n =  PV _ RT  =   (2.0 ×  10  5 )  ×0.020  _____________  8.31 ×  (22 + 273.16)    = 1.6mol .

The mass is then given by M = n M  m  = 1.6 ×  (28.8 ×  10   −3 )  = 0.046kg/46g   

The volume of the combined gas remains at 0.020m3.

The additional number of moles added is equal to n =  PV _ RT  =   (1.0 ×  10  5 )  × 2.0  ____________  8.31 ×  (22 + 273.16)   = 81.5mol

Using P =  nRT _ V  =   (1.6 + 81.5)  × 8.31 ×  (22 + 273.16)    ________________  0.020  = 1.0 × 10  4 Pa
