Previously, it was shown that for a mass-spring system, a = −  kx _ m  , where k represents the spring stiffness, and m, the mass.

Remember, that the negative sign indicates that the acceleration is in the opposite direction to the displacement as shown in the diagram.

We can directly compare the general equation of SHM, a = − ω  2 x with a = −  kx _ m  .

Equating coefficients, gives ω  2 =  k _ m 

Substituting ω =  2π _ T  into the above, gives (  2π _ T  )  2 =  k _ m  . This results in T = 2π √ __   m _ k    .

The above equation is the standard equation to calculate the time period T, of an oscillating mass-spring system. It is important to be familiar with this equation.

By inspection of the equation, it can be seen that:

Increasing the mass, will increase the period

Increasing the spring stiffness, will decrease the period.
