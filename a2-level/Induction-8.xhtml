Remembering that the EMF, ε is given by ε = −  d (Nɸ)  ___ dt  , the EMF at any instant is given by the negative gradient of the tangent.

By inspection of the graph on the right, the following facts emerge:

Zero EMF when coil is perpendicular to the field (zero gradient)

Max EMF when coil is parallel to the field (max gradient)

The magnetic flux linkage through the rotating coil below, is given by Nɸ = BANcosωt .

Maximum flux linkage occurs when ωt = 0°, where the coil is perpendicular to the field, and zero when ωt = 90°, where the coil is parallel to the field.

Since ω = 2πf, then the flux linkage can also be expressed as Nɸ= BANcos (2πf) .
