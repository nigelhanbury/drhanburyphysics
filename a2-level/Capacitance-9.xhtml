Determination of Initial Charge and Current

When the switch is in position A, the capacitor will be charged, and the pd across the capacitor when fully charged will equal the supply EMF, V0. In addition, the final charge is given by Q0.

At the instant the switch is repositioned to B, the capacitor will begin discharging through the resistor, R. The values, V0 and Q0 are then known as the initial charge and pd.

Since the capacitor and resistor are connected in parallel, they must have the exact same pd, V at all times. We can use this fact in order to determine the initial current, I0 through the resistor.

Since the initial pd across the capacitor is V0, then this must be the initial pd across the resistor, R.

Therefore, the initial current through R, can be determined using I  0 =   V  0  __ R  .

The initial charge is given by Q  0 = C V  0 .

Using V =  Q _ C  , we can see that V ∝ Q . In other words, as the capacitor discharges, Q will decrease, and so, V will decrease. This means that the pd across the resistor will decrease as the capacitor discharges.

Therefore, using I =  V _ R  , we can see that the current, I through the resistor must also decrease as the capacitor discharges.
