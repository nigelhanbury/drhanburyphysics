Using the Right Hand Grip Rule on the top conductor, the magnetic field is going into the page at point P, and similarly, for the bottom conductor, the magnetic field is acting out of the page.

We can make use of B =   μ  0 I __ 2πr  and simplify our calculations by recognising that point P is equidistant from both conductors.

The field due to the top conductor is given by B  5A =  4π × 10 − 7 × 5  ___________ 2π × 0.05  = 2.0 × 10   − 5 T .

Since point P is the same distance from the bottom conductor, and the current is double that of the top conductor, then the field due to the bottom conductor must be given by B  10A = 2 × 2.0 × 10   − 5 T = 4.0 × 10   − 5 T .

The resultant magnetic flux density must be B10A − B5A = 2.0 × 10   − 5 T acting out of the page.

Using F =   μ  0 I  1 I  2 L ____ 2πr  , gives F =  4π × 10 − 7 × 5 × 10 × 2.0   ___________  2π × 0.10   = 2.0 ×  10   − 4 N . The force is attractive.
