A well insulated container contains 2.5kg of liquid. The liquid is heated by a 4.0kW power supply over a 3 minute period. A graph of temperature against time was drawn.

Determine the specific heat capacity of the liquid.

Example Problem

Solution

It is important to not rote learn the formula on the previous page.

We can use Q = mcΔθ , where Q is equal to 4.0 × 10  3 × Δt , which represents the heat transferred in time, Δt .

Then, 4.0 × 10  3 × Δt = 2.5 × c × Δθ

After re-arranging, gives c = (  4.0 ×  10  3  _____ 2.5  ) × (  Δt _ Δθ  ) . We can see that  Δt _ Δθ  =  1 _ gradient  .

Example Problem

A 680nm laser is incident on a steel block of dimensions 30cm x 20cm x 0.50cm for 3 minutes. The rate at which photons are incident on the steel is 5.0 × 10  20 s  −1 . Specific heat capacity of steel is 420Jkg−1K−1 and density is 8100kgm−3.

Determine:

The incident power of the laser

The increase in temperature of the steel over 3 minutes

Comment on the validity of the above result.

Solution

Energy per photon is given by,  hc _ λ  =   (6.63 ×  10  -34 )  ×  (3.0 ×  10  8 )   __________________  680 ×  10  -9   = 2.9 × 10  −19 J .

Power is determined by (5.0 ×  10  20 ) × (2.9 ×  10  −19 ) = 150W .

Mass is given by, m = (0.30 × 0.20 × 0.0050) × 8100 = 2.4kg .

Using Q = mcΔθ , gives 150 × 180 = 2.4 × 420 × Δθ ⇒ Δθ = 27°C

The increase in temperature is 27°C.

In reality, the rise in temperature would be smaller as a proportion of the laser light will be reflected, along with the steel block transferring heat to the environment.
