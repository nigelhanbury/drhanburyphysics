Magnetic Flux Linkage

Consider a solenoid (coil) placed within a magnetic field as shown on the right.

The magnetic flux 𝛷, passing through the coil is given by

𝛷 = BA , where A, represents the cross sectional area of the coil. Here, the lines of flux are perpendicular to A.

Since there is a conducting wire loop forming the perimeter of this area, the magnetic flux is linking with this conductor, and so, forming a magnetic flux linkage.

Since there are N turns of wire that form this coil, this flux is linking with each of the N loops of wire.

Therefore, we can define Flux linkage as N𝛷 , where N𝛷 = BAN . Units: Wb or Wb turn

Definition of Magnetic Flux Linkage

N × magnetic flux
