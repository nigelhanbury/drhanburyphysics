Unit 4, Section 2: Electrostatic Fields of Force

Diagram A on the right, shows the electric field pattern around two positive charges. At a point equidistant between the charges, the electric field strength due to each charge are equal and opposite, and so, cancel. This produces a zero field strength, and the field lines are drawn far apart.

Diagram B shows the electric field pattern around two equal and opposite charges. At a point equidistant between the charges, the electric field strength due to each charge are in the same direction, and so, re-enforce each other. This produces a greater field strength, and the field lines are drawn closer together.
