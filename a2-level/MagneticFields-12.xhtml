Key learning points regarding Hall Voltage equation:

The equations are not given in the data booklet, so make sure you learn them and remember it.

You should be able to derive them – just remember the principles and the equations should follow.

You will be expected to correctly add a Voltmeter to a diagram of a Hall probe; it should be added between the positive and negative charged faces.

The thickness d, of the wafer is made as thin as possible in order to maximise the Hall Voltage, VH as V  H ∝  1 _ d  .

VH is proportional to B for constant I. Therefore, the Hall probe can be used to investigate the strength of a magnetic field.
