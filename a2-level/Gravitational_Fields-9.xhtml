Equipotential Lines

Equipotential lines are lines of constant gravitational potential. Referring to the equation for gravitational potential, V  g = −  GM _ r  , all points lying along a circular path centred on the planet have constant r, and hence, constant gravitational potential.

The difference in potential ∆V, between each equipotential line must be constant. This results in a series of concentric circles where the distance between each one increases as we move away from the planet.

The work required to move a mass m, from one equipotential line to another, is simply the difference between the two potentials ∆V multiplied by the mass m, W = m∆V .

The field lines must be perpendicular to the equipotential lines.
