Total Kinetic energy of One Mole of Gas

Since the mean kinetic energy of a particle is given by  1 _ 2  m _  c  2 =  3 _ 2  kT , we can calculate the total kinetic energy of 1 mole of gas.

The total energy E, is given by E = N  A (  3 _ 2  kT) , where N  A , represents the number of particles in 1 mole.

Substituting k =  R __  N  A   into E = N  A (  3 _ 2  kT) , gives E =  3 _ 2  RT .

Total Kinetic energy of n Moles of Gas

For a gas comprising n moles, the total kinetic energy U, can now be calculated.

Therefore, U = n ×  3 _ 2  RT , which after re-arranging, gives U =  3 _ 2  nRT .

Making use of PV = nRT , gives U =  3 _ 2  PV .

The variable U, is introduced here for total translational kinetic energy, as in the next topic, internal energy is discussed, and this is the standard symbol.

Key equations:

E =  3 _ 2  RT 1 mole

U =  3 _ 2  nRT n moles

The equation, U =  3 _ 2  PV is not within the WJEC specification, but is, however, extremely useful.
