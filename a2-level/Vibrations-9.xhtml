Velocity

Referring to the diagram on the right, the magnitude of the velocity vector of the mass is given by v = rω . The velocity of the shadow V  S , is the component of v, in the x direction. Therefore, V  S = vcos (ωt) , and so, V  S = rωcos (ωt) .

Here, we can see that the maximum velocity of the shadow v  max , is given by v  max = rω . This occurs when t = 0, as the shadow passes through the equilibrium position.

In other words, v  max , for an oscillating particle is simply the linear speed of the tip of a rotating vector of length r. If r = A, then we get the familiar v  max = ωA .

Acceleration

Referring to the diagram on the right, the acceleration of the mass acts towards the centre of rotation, and is given by a = r ω  2 . The acceleration of the shadow aS, is equal to the component of a, acting along the x direction.

Therefore, a  S = asin (ωt) , and so, a  S = r ω  2 sin (ωt) .

Since aS, acts along the negative x direction, we must state a  S = − r ω  2 sin (ωt) .

Here, we can see that the maximum acceleration of the shadow, amax is a  max = r ω  2 .

If r = A, then we get the familiar a  max = Aω  2 .

Comparing x = rsin (ωt) with a  S = − r ω  2 sin (ωt) , we get a  S = − ω  2 x . This demonstrates that the shadow oscillates with SHM.
