During the first half cycle, the diaphragm moves inwards and decreases the distance between the capacitor plates. State and explain the direction of electron flow.

During the second half cycle, the diaphragm moves outwards and increases the distance between the capacitor plates. State and explain the direction of electron flow.

A 10MΩ resistor is used in the circuit. Determine the value of the capacitance, C in order that a 10kHz sound wave can be converted to a 10kHz output signal.

Solution

As C ∝  1 _ d  , the capacitance increases during the first half cycle. Therefore, the capacitor is able to store more charge for the same applied pd. The capacitor will now start charging with electrons moving in an anticlockwise direction around the circuit.

As C ∝  1 _ d  , the capacitance decreases during the second half cycle. Therefore, the capacitor is not able to store as much charge for the same applied pd. The capacitor will now start discharging with electrons moving in a clockwise direction around the circuit.

In order to get the best output signal, the capacitor must be able to fully charge and discharge during one complete cycle.

The period, T is given by T =  1 _____ 10 ×  10  3   = 1.0 × 10   − 4 s .
