Notice that it has negative values and positive values. This is due to two reasons:

The work done in bringing a unit positive charge from infinity is negative for the negatively charged sphere and positive for the positively charged sphere.

The system (electric field) is doing the work in the case of the negatively charged sphere and an external agent is doing the work for the case of a positively charged sphere.

Definition of Electrical Potential

Electrical potential at a point EV, is the work done per unit charge in bringing a positive charge from infinity to that point.

Unit: V or JC − 1.

Electrical potential energy at a point EP is defined as the work done in bringing a charge q, from infinity to that point.

In other words, E  P = q V  E and has units of J. This too, is a scalar quantity.

Definition of Electrical Potential Energy

Electrical potential energy at a point EP, is defined as the work done in bringing a charge q, from infinity to that point.

Unit: J .

The electrical potential inside a charged sphere is constant, and is equal to the electrical potential at the surface. The electrical potential at the surface is equal to the work done in bringing a unit positive charge from infinity to the surface.

Since the electric field inside the sphere is zero, the unit positive charge experiences zero force, and hence, no further work is required.

Electric potential at a point is the work done per unit charge in bringing a positive charge from infinity to that point. Unit: V or JC − 1. The electrical potential is defined to be zero at infinity. It is a scalar quantity.
