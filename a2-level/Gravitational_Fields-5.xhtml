The Equivalence of Gravitational Field Strength and Acceleration Due to Gravity

We can make use of F = ma to show that the numerical value of gravitational field strength g, is exactly the same as that of the acceleration of free fall, a. In other words, all masses at the surface of a planet will accelerate towards the ground at the same rate.

Consider a mass m, just above the surface of a planet as shown to the left. The resultant force F, acting on the mass is its weight mg.

The acceleration a, can be calculated using a =  F _ m  =  mg _ m  = g . This shows that all masses accelerate at exactly the same rate and equal to g.

The field strength at the surface of the Earth is 9.81Nkg − 1, and so, all objects at the surface of the Earth will free fall at 9.81ms − 2.
