The ion beam is deflected and exits the field at point B. Each ion entering at point A has a velocity of 5.0 × 10  5 m s   − 1 , and a charge of +2e. The molar mass of Pb  2+ is 207g mol   − 1 .

Determine the velocity of the ions at point B and explain your answer

The radius of the path followed by the ion beam is 25cm. Determine the required centripetal force acting on each Lead ion

Determine the magnetic flux density required in order to achieve given radius of curvature

If the Lead ions were replaced by a stream of beta minus particles entering point A at the same velocity as above, describe the path followed within the field. No calculations required.

Solution

The velocity at point B is 5.0 × 10  5 m s   − 1 . The magnetic force acting on the ion is always perpendicular to the velocity. In other words, there is no component of the force acting in the direction of motion (along tangent), and so, no work is done on the ion. Since no work is done, there is no change in kinetic energy, and hence, no change in speed.

Firstly, we need to determine the mass of the ion. m =  207 ×  10   − 3  ________  N  A   =  207 ×  10   − 3   _________ 6.02 ×  10  23   = 3.44 × 10   − 25 kg .

Now, we can use F =  m v  2  ___ r  =   (3.44 ×  10   − 25 )  ×  (5.0 ×  10  5 )  2    __________________  0.25  = 3.44 × 10   − 13 N .

The magnetic force is providing the required centripetal force. Therefore, 3.44 × 10   − 13 = BQv .

We can then write 3.44 × 10   − 13 = B × 2 × (1.6 ×  10   − 19 ) × (5.0 ×  10  5 ) . This leads to B = 2.15T .

Since we are dealing with a flow of negative charge moving to the right (towards A), this is equivalent to conventional current acting to the left. Making use of FLHR, the magnetic force would act vertically downwards at point A. This indicates that the beta particles would perform circular motion by deflecting downwards in a clockwise fashion. Since the mass of the beta particles is significantly less than that of the Lead ions, along with experiencing half the magnetic force, the radius of curvature must be much smaller.
