Example Problem

Find the volume occupied by 1.0mol of an ideal gas at standard temperature (293K) and pressure ( 1.0 × 10  5 Pa ).

Solution

Using PV = nRT ⇒ V =  nRT _ P  =  1.0 × 8.31 × 293  ____________ 1.0 ×  10  5   = 0.024 m  3

Example Problem

A car tyre contains 0.020m3 of air at 20°C and pressure 3.0 × 10  5 Pa . Find the mass of air in each tyre. (The molar mass of air is 28.8gmol-1)

Solution

Firstly, we need to calculate the number of moles, n =  PV _ RT  =   (3.0 ×  10  5 )  × 0.020  _____________  8.31 ×  (20 + 273.16)   = 2.4mol

We can now use M = n M  m = 2.4 × 28.8 = 69g
