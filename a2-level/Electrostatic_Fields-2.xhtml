Electric Field Strength

Electric field strength is defined as the force experienced per unit charge by a small positive charge placed in the field. Unit: V m   − 1 or N C   − 1 .

The unit V m   − 1 is not obvious at this stage and will be derived later.

The electric field strength E, formula is valid for charged spheres. In this case, r is the distance to the centre of the charged sphere. It is important to note, that there is no electric field inside a charged sphere, and that all of the charge resides on the surface only.
