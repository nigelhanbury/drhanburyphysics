Example Problem

A block is placed on a platform, which is made to oscillate via a motor as shown in the diagram. The amplitude of the oscillation is 10cm.

The frequency of the motor is slowly increased from zero. Determine the minimum frequency at the point the block will lose contact with the platform.

Solution

In order for the block to lose contact with the platform, the platform’s acceleration must act downwards. Therefore, maximum acceleration downwards occurs when the platform is at its highest point. The platform’s downwards acceleration must equal free-fall in order for the block to lose contact.

Therefore, g = (2πf)  2 A . This gives us 9.81 = (2πf)  2 × 0.10 ⇒ f = 1.58Hz
