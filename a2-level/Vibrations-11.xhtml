The total energy E  T , is given by E  T = E  K + E  P .

Owing to the conservation of energy, the decrease in elastic potential energy must equal the increase in kinetic energy. Here, maximum kinetic energy occurs when the mass passes through the equilibrium position.

The interchange between kinetic and potential energy is true for all systems that oscillate with SHM.

For an oscillating pendulum, the interchange is between kinetic and gravitational potential energy.

Notice, that the kinetic and potential energy on the left oscillate with twice the frequency of the oscillation itself.

In other words, both energies peak twice within one cycle.
