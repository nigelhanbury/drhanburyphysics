Binary System Moving Away (receding) from Observer

The diagram below, represents a planet - star system that is moving away from the observer at a velocity v  rec . For simplicity, only the radial velocity component, v  rad    has been drawn. Since the whole system is moving (receding) away from the observer, we have to vectorally add v  rec to the previous radial velocity component as shown. The following graphs show how the radial velocity and measured wavelength vary with time.

As can be seen, the effect of the recessional velocity, v  rec is to displace the radial velocity graph upwards by v  rec . Therefore, the recessional velocity of the system can be determined by the mean value of the maximum and minimum values, eg, v  rec =   v  max  +  v  min  ______ 2  . The orbital velocity is simply the “amplitude”, and given by v  o = v  max − v  rec .

Key equations:

v  rec =   v  max  +  v  min  ______ 2 

v  o = v  max − v  rec
