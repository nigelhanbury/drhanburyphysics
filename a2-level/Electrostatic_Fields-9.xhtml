Solution

Recognising that each triangle is a 3, 4, 5 triangle, the lengths AC and BC must be 5.0m.

Using V =  Q ___ 4𝜋 𝜀  0 r  , the electrical potential at point C due to A is given by V  A = 9.0 × 10  9 ×  20 ×  10   − 6  _____ 5.0  = 3.6 × 10  4 V .

The electrical potential at point C due to B is given by V  B = 9.0 × 10  9 ×  20 ×  10   − 6  _____ 5.0  = 3.6 × 10  4 V .

Since the electrical potential is a scalar quantity, we can just add the values to find the total. V  E = V  A + V  B = 2 × (3.6 ×  10  4 ) = 7.2 × 10  4 V .

The stored potential energy is given by E  P = q × V  E = (1.6 ×  10   − 19 ) × (7.2 ×  10  4 ) = 1.2 × 10   − 14 J .

We can use the conservation of energy. Since we can consider the electrical potential energy to be zero very far away, there is a decrease in potential energy.

Decrease in potential energy = increase in kinetic energy, therefore, Δ E  P + Δ E  K = 0 . We can write 1.2 × 10   − 14 =  1 _ 2  × (1.67 ×  10   − 27 ) × v  2 . This gives v = 3.8 × 10  6 m s   − 1 .

Solution

Radius of A is 1.0m, so we can use − 8.0 × 10  4 = 9.0 × 10  9 ×   Q  1  __ 1.0  . This gives Q  1 = − 8.9 × 10   − 6 C or − 8.9µC.

Radius of B is 1.0m, so we can use + 9.0 × 10  4 = 9.0 × 10  9 ×   Q  2  __ 1.0  . This gives Q  2 = + 1.0 × 10   − 5 C or + 10µC.

The position is 4.8m from the centre of A, as this is the point where V  A = − 1.7 × 10  4 V and V  B = + 1.7 × 10  4 V .

The total potential at surface of A is V  A = (− 8.0 + 1.0) × 10  4 = − 7.0 × 10  4 V and at surface of B is V  B = (− 0.9 + 9.0) × 10  4 = + 8.1 × 10  4 V .
