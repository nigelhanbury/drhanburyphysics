Example Problem

A toy car completes a lap of a circular track in 12s. The radius of the track is 0.5m. Find:

The speed of the toy car

The centripetal acceleration

Using v =  2πr _ t  =  2π × 0.5 _ 12  = 0.26m s  −1

Using a =   v  2  __ r  =   0.26  2  __ 0.5  = 0.14m s  −2

Example Problem

A mass on the end of a string 0.6m long is whirled around with a frequency of 5 revolutions every second. Find the speed of the mass.

Solution

Using v = 2πrf = 2π × 0.6 × 5 = 18.8m s  −1

Example Problem

The distance of the Earth from the Sun is 152 million kilometres. Determine:

The speed of the Earth in its orbit

The Earth’s centripetal acceleration

The centripetal force acting on the Earth and state what is providing this force. The mass of the Earth is 6.25 × 10  24 kg .

Solution

Using v =  2πr _ t  =  2π × 152 ×  10  9   _________  365 × 24 × 3600  = 3.03 × 10  4 m s  -1

Using a =   v  2  __ r  =   (3.03 ×  10  4 )  2  _______  152 ×  10  9    = 6.04 × 10  −3 m s  −2

Using F = ma = 6.25 × 10  24 × 6.04 × 10  −3 = 3.78 × 10  22 N . Gravitational force acting on Earth due to Sun responsible for this force.
