Hadrons

Hadrons are particles that are made up of quarks, (q).

Definition: Hadrons are particles consisting of quarks or antiquarks bound together. Only hadrons (and quarks or antiquarks themselves) can feel the strong force.

Quarks have a fractional value of charge ,i.e., the up quark has a charge of +  2 _ 3  e , and the down quark has a charge of −  1 _ 3  e . Quarks must combine in such a way that the charge on the composite particle is an integer multiple of e, where e = −1.6 × 10  -19 C .

Baryons

Baryons are made of three quarks (q q q), and anti-baryons are made of three anti-quarks ( _ q _ q _ q ). Although there are many types of hadron, only two, protons and neutrons, are found in ordinary matter. This is because most hadrons are very unstable and only exist very briefly before decaying into other particles. Protons and neutrons are the most common examples of baryons. Since protons and neutrons make up the nucleus of an atom, they are also referred to as nucleons.
