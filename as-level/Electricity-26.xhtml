Cells and Batteries

The diagram on the right, represents a cell and a battery connected to a bulb. One way to increase the supply EMF to a circuit is to connect cells together in series. More than one cell is known as a battery.

In this particular example, a cell has an EMF of 1.5V. The battery has an EMF of 3 x 1.5V or 4.5V.

It is important to connect the cells together properly. The positive terminal of one cell needs to be connected to the negative terminal of another. If one cell is connected the wrong way around, then that cell acts as a negative EMF.

If we add more cells to increase the supply EMF, the internal resistance of the battery will increase.

The power dissipated in the cell or battery is the power dissipated in the internal resistance.
