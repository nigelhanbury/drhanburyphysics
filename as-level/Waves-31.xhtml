The diagram shows an empty container sat on top of a semi - circular glass block. An LED produces a ray of light that is totally internally reflected. The detector produces a signal when the reflected light is incident upon it. [ Refractive index of glass is 1.5 ]

Verify that the ray will be totally internally reflected

When the container is filled with water, the detector signal decreases. Explain this with the aid of calculation. [ Refractive index of water is 1.32 ]

Solution

Firstly, we need to calculate the critical angle as before.

Using sinθ  C =   n  2  __  n  1   , gives sin θ  C =  1 _ 1.5  , resulting in θ  C = 41.8° .

Since the angle of incidence is greater than the critical angle, total internal reflection will occur.

We need to determine the new critical angle.

Using sinθ  C =   n  2  __  n  1   , gives sin θ  C =  1.32 _ 1.5  , resulting in θ  C = 61.6° .

Since the angle of incidence is now less than the critical angle, total internal reflection will not occur, and the ray will be refracted at the glass/water boundary. This will result in a faint reflected ray incident on the detector, and hence, a drop in signal strength.
