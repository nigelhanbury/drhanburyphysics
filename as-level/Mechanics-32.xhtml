Definition of The Principle of Conservation of Momentum

The vector sum of the momenta (plural) of bodies in a system stays constant even if forces act between the bodies, provided there is no external resultant force.

Definition of Inelastic Collision

A collision in which total KE is lost.

Definition of Elastic Collision

A collision in which there is no change in total KE.
