Example Problem

A 4.0mW laser emitting light of wavelength 658nm is incident normally on an area of 3.0 × 10  -6 m  2    .

Area, A = 3.0 × 10  -6 m  2

658nm laser

Determine the energy of a single photon.

Determine the momentum of a single photon.

If the laser light is perfectly reflected, determine the mean force exerted on the material, and hence, the pressure.

Solution

Using E =  hc _ λ  , gives E =  6.63 ×  10  -34  × 3 ×  10  8      _______________  658 ×  10  -9   = 3.0 × 10  -19 J .

Using p =  h _ λ  , gives p =  6.63 ×  10  -34  ________ 658 ×  10  -9   = 1.0 × 10  -27 kgm s  -1 .

Firstly, we need to determine the number of photons per second incident on the area, n =  4.0 ×  10  -3  _______ 3.0 ×  10  -19   = 1.3 × 10  16 s  -1 .

The change in momentum is equal to Δp = 2p = 2.0 × 10  -27 kgm s  -1 .

Therefore, F = nΔp = 1.3 × 10  16 × 2.0 × 10  -27 = 2.6 × 10  -11 N .

The pressure, P is given by P =  F _ A  =  2.6 × 10  -11   ________ 3.0 ×  10  -6   = 8.7 × 10  -6 Pa .

Example Problem

The setup below, shows a demonstration of the wave like behaviour of electrons. An electron gun accelerates electrons through a voltage of 4kV, which subsequently pass through a thin layer of graphite. [Mass of electron is me = 9.1 × 10  -31 kg ]

Determine the velocity of the electrons after they emerge from the gun.

Determine the momentum of the electrons, and hence, the corresponding de Broglie wavelength.

Describe and explain how the diffraction rings will change if the voltage was further increased.

Solution

We can use eV =  1 _ 2  m v  2 . Therefore, 1.6 × 10  -19 × 4000 =  1 _ 2  × 9.1 × 10  -31 × v  2 .

This gives v = 3.8 × 10  7 m s  -1 .

The momentum, p = 9.1 × 10  -31 × 3.8 × 10  7 = 3.5 × 10  -23 kgm s  -1 .

We can use p =  h _ λ  , and so, 3.5 × 10  -23 =  6.63 ×  10  -34  ______ λ  , therefore, λ = 1.9 × 10  -11 m .

Increasing the voltage will increase the momentum of the electrons, and hence, there will be a decrease in the de Broglie wavelength compared to the inter atomic spacing. This will result in less diffraction, and the distance between the diffraction rings will decrease.
