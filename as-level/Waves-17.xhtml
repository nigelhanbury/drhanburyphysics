The diagram below, represents two loudspeakers connected to a signal generator. Each loudspeaker acts as a point source of waves. The distance between the two loudspeakers is 0.40m.

Signal generator

Explain why the above setup ensures the two loudspeakers produce a coherent source of sound waves

Explain why the microphone at position A, records maximum signal strength

The microphone is slowly moved from position A to position B. The signal strength decreases to a minimum. Beyond B, the signal strength slowly increases. Explain why the signal strength changes in this way

Determine the path difference between the two sound waves arriving at B. [It is not valid to use the Young’s double slit formula, as the microphone is not far away from the speakers]

Determine the frequency of the signal generator. [speed of sound is 330m s  -1 ]

The frequency of the signal generator is slowly increased until the signal strength recorded by the microphone at position B reaches a maximum. Determine the new frequency.

Solution

As the two speakers are connected to the same output of the signal generator, the two speakers produce waves of the same frequency, wavelength and constant phase difference.

At position A, the path difference SA −RA = 0, and therefore, the two waves arrive at A in phase and superpose to produce constructive interference and maximum amplitude.

Moving away from A, introduces a path difference between the two waves arriving at the microphone, and the waves become out of phase, resulting in a decrease in amplitude of the resultant wave until at B, the two waves are 180° out of phase. At B, total destructive interference occurs, producing minimum amplitude.

The path difference, pd = RB − SB . RB = √ ________   1.10  2  +  2.50  2   = 2.73m and SB = √ ________   0.70  2  +  2.50  2   = 2.60m . pd = 2.73 − 2.60 = 0.13m.

Since for the first minima, pd =  λ _ 2  , it follows that 0.13 =  λ _ 2  , and so, λ = 0.26m. We can write 330 = f × 0.26 , which gives f = 1270Hz.

At maximum signal strength, pd = 1λ , therefore, λ = 0.13m. Since the wavelength has halved, the frequency has doubled, therefore, f = 2 × 1270 = 2540Hz .
