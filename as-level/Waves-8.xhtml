Example Problem

The solid curve below, represents a snapshot of a sound wave with a wavelength of 0.33m. The broken curve represents a snapshot of the wave 0.38ms later.

Determine:

The speed of sound

The frequency of the sound wave

Solution

From the graph, we can see that 0.33m corresponds to 8 squares. Therefore 1 square =  0.33 _ 8  = 0.041m .

The peak has moved 3 squares, corresponding to 3 × 0.041 = 0.12m .

Since the wave has travelled 0.12m in 0.38ms, the speed of the wave can be calculated as v =  0.12 ______ 0.38 ×  10  -3   = 316m s  -1 .

We can use v = fλ , giving 316 = f × 0.33 , resulting in f = 958Hz.
