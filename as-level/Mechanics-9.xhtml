Definition of Density

Density is defined as the mass per unit volume,

S.I. Unit: kgm-3

If the density of a material is 100kgm-3, then 1m3 of that material has a mass of 100kg.

Notice, that the unit of density is in terms of S.I. base units [kg] & [m].

Measuring the Density of an Irregular Object

Without the measuring cylinder in place, fill the displacement can to overflowing.

When water stops dripping from the spout, place the measuring cylinder in position.

Gently lower the object into the water until it is fully submerged.

When the water stops dripping from the spout, the volume of water in the measuring cylinder is equal to the volume of the object.

Measure the mass of the object using a digital balance.
