Solution

The component does not obey Ohm's law as it is not a straight line through the origin

Filament lamp

Calculating the resistance at points 1, 2 and 3, gives R  1 =  2.0 _ 0.2  = 10Ω , R  2 =  4.0 _ 0.32  = 12.5Ω and R  3 =  6.0 _ 0.39  = 15.4Ω . Therefore, the resistance increases as the voltage increases.

As the voltage increases, the current increases and results in an increase in temperature due to an increase in the frequency of collisions between the free electrons and metal ions, which results in the metal ions oscillating with greater amplitude and frequency. This reduces the mean drift velocity, and hence, current for the same applied voltage. Therefore, the resistance increases with temperature.
