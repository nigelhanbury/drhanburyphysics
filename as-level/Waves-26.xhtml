Example Problem

In order to create an ultrasonic grating, a glass cell is utilised to hold a liquid, where a Piezo-electric crystal is attached to one side of the wall. Ultrasonic waves are generated from the crystal and travel towards a reflector positioned on the opposite wall. Once the waves reflect off the reflector, they superpose with the incident waves and create a longitudinal standing wave pattern. The antinodes of the stationary wave refract light very little, and behave as slits in a conventional diffraction grating.
