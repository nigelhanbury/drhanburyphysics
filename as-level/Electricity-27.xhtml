Determine the emf of the cell.

Determine the internal resistance of the cell.

Determine the maximum current through the cell and state its significance.

Determine the load resistance, which results in a current of 2.0A.

Determine the efficiency of the cell when there is a current of 2.0A.

The emf of the cell is given by the vertical intercept, and so, E = 9.3V.

The internal resistance is given by the negative gradient, r = − (  0 − 9.3 _ 9.3 − 0  ) = 1.0Ω .

The maximum current is 9.3A. This is the current that passes through the cell when the load resistance is zero. In other words, its the current on short circuit.

From the graph, the pd across the load at 2.0A is 7.3V. Therefore, the load resistance is given by R =  V _ I  =  7.3 _ 2.0  = 3.65Ω .

We can use, efficiency =  Useful power transferred to load   ___________   Power delivered by cell 

Useful power, P  L = IV = 2.0 × 7.3 = 14.6W .

Power delivered, P  D = IE = 2.0 × 9.3 = 18.6W .

Therefore, the efficiency is given by   P  L  __  P  D   =  14.6 _ 18.6  = 0.78 or 78%.
