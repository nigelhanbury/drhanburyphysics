Calculating the Resultant or Net Moment

The diagram on the right, represents a massless rod sat on a pivot with several forces acting on the rod. For simplicity, we have made the forces perpendicular to the rod. In this situation, we need to be able to determine the resultant or net moment. Remember, we can take moments about any point.

The resultant moment about a stated point is simply the sum of the individual moments about that same point.

Notice, that there is a reaction force acting on the rod due to the pivot.

It is very important to carefully decide which point we should take moments about. In the example above, four forces are acting on the rod. However, with a sensible point chosen, we can reduce the number of forces used to three. Here, we have chosen that stated point to be at the location of the pivot. It is important to take into account the rotational sense of the moment. In this case, we have chosen positive values of the moment, Fd to represent anti-clockwise and negative values to represent clockwise. Should the resultant be positive, the resultant moment about the pivot would be anti-clockwise and the rod would rotate with the left side of the rod moving down.
