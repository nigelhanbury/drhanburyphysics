Definition of Refractive Index

The refractive index, n, is defined as the ratio of the speed of light in a vacuum to the speed of light in a material/medium, n = c/V, where c is the speed of light in a vacuum and v, the speed of light in a medium. The refractive index of a vacuum is therefore, 1.

When a ray passes into a medium of higher refractive index, the ray refracts towards the normal and when a ray passes into a medium of lower refractive index, the ray refracts away from the normal.
