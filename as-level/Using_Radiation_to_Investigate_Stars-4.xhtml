Example Problem

The surface temperature of a neutron star is typically 50000K. Determine the wavelength of the peak spectral intensity and state which part of the electromagnetic spectrum this occupies.

Solution

λ  max =  2.9 ×  10  -3  _____ 50000  = 5.8 × 10  -8 m = 58nm

This corresponds to the Ultraviolet region of the EM spectrum.
