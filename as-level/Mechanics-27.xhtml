Experiment to Determine Acceleration of Free-fall

The apparatus below, can be used to determine the acceleration of free fall, g. With the power supply switch in position 1, the current flows through the electromagnet and holds the steel ball in place. The magnetic field produced by the electromagnet magnetises the steel ball, therefore, the ball is attracted to the electromagnet. When the power supply switch is moved to position 2, the current supplying the electromagnet is switched off. At this point, the electronic timer is started and the steel ball is released. When the steel ball hits the hinged gate, the circuit containing the electronic timer is broken and the timer stops.

Method

With both the electromagnet switch and the gate switch closed, attach a steel ball bearing on the end of the electromagnet.

Use a metre rule to measure the height, h, between the bottom of the steel ball bearing and the gate switch.

When the electromagnet switch is opened the steel ball is released and the timer automatically starts

When the ball hits and opens the gate switch, the timer automatically stops

Adjust the position of the electromagnet to change the height h. Measure the time, t for a range of values of h.

Take at least 3 readings for each value of h and find a mean value for t.
