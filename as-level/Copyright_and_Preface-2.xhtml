Intended Audience

This book covers the contents of the WJEC Units 1 & 2 A Level specification. The book is aimed at those students wishing to gain a more in depth covarage of the theory and focuses on the understanding of topics from the ground up. There are no exam style questions as the author does not wish to teach to the exams, rather, the author wants to focus on understanding so that the student can think for themselves and be able to answer a wide range of questions and not neccesarily those from just one exam board. Throughout the book, there are many illistrations to help the student visualise difficult concepts along with sample problems and solutions to help consolidate learning.

This book is based on the WJEC A Level specification 2015 and does not imply in any way that the contents have been approved nor endorsed by the WJEC examination board.
