1.1 Basic Physics

Physics is a physical science, and as such, the measurement of physical quantities plays an important role in order to build and formulate theories. Initially, we will look at the units these physical quantities have and break them down into SI base units and derived units.

Physical Quantities

In order to compare quantities, such as mass, we need to have a defined standard in order to make the comparison. The system used is known as S.I. (Le Systéme International d’unités), and is the most widely used system of measurement.

Derived Units

Many of the variables used in physics, and indeed everyday life, are not the S.I. base units. These units are expressed in terms of the base units. One example of this is speed or velocity.
